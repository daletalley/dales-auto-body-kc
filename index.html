The Craft

Seven coats of patience.

A finish that looks a foot deep is built one thin layer at a time. Scroll to build it.

01 / 07Bare steel
  1. 01

    Tear down & read the metal

    Trim off, panels out, everything under bright light. You can’t fix what you haven’t found — so we find all of it before a single number goes on the estimate.

  2. 02

    Straighten, don’t hide

    Frame and panels get pulled and hammered back to true. Filler is a skim, not a sculpture — the shape comes from the steel.

  3. 03

    Prime & block

    Epoxy seals the metal, then high-build primer gets block-sanded by hand until the reflections run straight as a level.

  4. 04

    Lay the color

    Factory matches are tinted and sprayed out on test cards first. Custom colors get mixed until they look right in sunlight and shop light.

  5. 05

    Candy & flake

    Translucent candy goes on in even passes over a metallic base — every coat deeper than the last. Miss one pass and it shows, so nobody misses.

  6. 06

    Stripe by hand

    Graphics are taped and laid out on the bike, then outlined with a striping brush and a steady hand. No vinyl, no shortcuts.

  7. 07

    Clear, cut & buff

    Coats of clear, color-sanded flat and buffed back to glass. Then it gets checked under the lights one more time before the keys go back.

What we do

Body, paint, and soul.

  • Collision & frame

    Structural straightening, panel replacement, welding and rust repair — done so the car drives as straight as it looks.

  • Custom paint

    Candy, pearl, metal flake, two-tones and color changes. If you can picture it, we can mix it and lay it down.

  • Pinstripe & graphics

    Hand-laid flames, scallops, outlines and lettering. Real brush, real paint, one of one.

  • Upholstery & interiors

    Seats rebuilt and recovered, pleats, diamond stitch, headliners and carpet — stitched tight and built to be sat on.

  • Motorcycle customs

    Baggers, tanks, fenders, fairings and full builds. Stretched bags, color-matched dash, the works.

  • Insurance claims

    Honest estimates in plain English and in insurance-speak. We’ll walk you through what’s covered and what’s not.

Our Story

Same corner. Same hands. Fifty years.

Dale opened the doors on State Avenue in 1975, and the shop has been his ever since. No franchise, no call center, no corporate paint schedule — just a guy who loves the work, a booth, and a lot full of neighbors’ cars.

Over the years the jobs have ranged from fender-benders on the family sedan to full candy builds on show baggers. The approach has never changed: do it right, tell people the truth, and don’t hand back the keys until it’s something you’d put your name on.
